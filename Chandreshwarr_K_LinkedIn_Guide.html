Quick Wins — Do These Today

Profile Boosts
📸
Professional Profile Photo Use a clear, well-lit headshot with a neutral background. Profiles with photos get 21x more views. No selfies or group photos — just you, smiling, professional.
🖼️
Banner / Cover Image Add a custom banner behind your photo. Use a gaming/tech themed image — controller, game screen, dark tech aesthetic. Free tool: Canva.com → search "LinkedIn Banner" → use a free template. Size: 1584 × 396px.
🔗
Custom Profile URL Go to Edit public profile & URL (top right) → Edit your custom URL → set it to linkedin.com/in/chandreshwarr-k (remove the random numbers). Cleaner for resumes and emails.
✅
Open to Work Click "Open to" below your name → "Finding a new job" → select relevant job titles: Game QA Engineer, QA Tester, Senior QA Engineer, QA Lead, Test Engineer → set to "Recruiters only" so your current employer can't see it.
📣
Creator Mode / Featured Section Turn on Creator Mode (in settings) to get a "Follow" button. Add a Featured post — write a short post about your experience testing a live game event or Xbox compliance process. Visibility from posts drives recruiter inbound.
⭐
Request Recommendations Ask 2–3 colleagues or managers from SIDE/PTW or Deca Games to write you a LinkedIn recommendation. Even one strong recommendation dramatically increases recruiter trust. Send them a short template to make it easy for them.
🔔
Engage Weekly Like or comment on 3–5 posts per week from game studios, QA leads, or gaming industry accounts. LinkedIn's algorithm shows your profile to more people when you're active — even just reacting to posts counts.
