## Weekly Workout Plan

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| Monday | Workout A |
| Tuesday | 15–20 min easy walk outside |
| Wednesday | Workout B |
| Thursday | Rest or 10 min stretching |
| Friday | Workout A |
| Saturday | 20–25 min walk |
| Sunday | Full rest |

---

### Warm-Up (5 min — do this before EVERY workout)

1. March in place — 1 min
2. Arm circles forward + back — 30 sec each
3. Hip circles — 30 sec each direction
4. Slow leg swings (hold wall) — 30 sec each leg

---

### Workout A — Full Body (2 rounds, 60 sec rest between rounds)

| Exercise | Reps | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Wall push-ups | 12 | Stand ~2 ft from wall, hands shoulder-width |
| Partial squats | 10 | Go only halfway down, slow and controlled |
| Glute bridges | 15 | Lie on back, feet flat, drive hips up, hold 1 sec at top |
| Standing knee raises | 20 (10 each leg) | March with knees to hip height |
| Superman hold | 8 | Lie face-down, lift arms + legs, hold 2 sec |
| Forearm plank | 20–30 sec | On elbows, no wrist load |

**Cool-down (5 min):** Quad stretch, seated hamstring stretch, child's pose, chest opener.

---

### Workout B — Lower Body + Core (2 rounds, 60 sec rest between rounds)

| Exercise | Reps | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Calf raises | 15 | Hold wall for balance if needed |
| Glute bridges (single leg) | 8 each | Skip if knee hurts |
| Side-lying leg raises | 12 each side | Lie on side, raise top leg to 45°, slow |
| Bird dog | 8 each side | Opposite arm + leg extended |
| Dead bug | 8 each side | Lie on back, extend opposite arm and leg slowly |
| Wall sit | 20–30 sec | Back against wall, knees as low as comfortable |

**Cool-down (5 min):** Same as Workout A.

---

### Month 2 Progression (when exercises feel easy for 2 straight weeks)

- Wall push-ups → Incline push-ups (hands on chair or table)
- Partial squats → Full squats
- Add 1 extra round to each workout
- Plank: increase by 5–10 sec each week

## Eating Guidelines

### 3 Rules to Follow

1. **Protein at every meal** — keeps you full, protects muscle, speeds fat loss.
2. **Drink water before you eat** — reduces overeating.
3. **Cut liquid calories first** — cold drinks, packaged juice, sweetened chai.

---

### What to Reduce (gradually)

| Instead of | Have this |
|-----------|-----------|
| Cold drinks / juice | Nimbu pani, chaas, plain water |
| Chips, namkeen daily | Roasted chana, peanuts (palm-size) |
| Heavy food after 10:30 PM | Light snack or nothing |
| Large rice / 3+ rotis | Smaller carb portion, more dal/sabzi/chicken |
| Fried food daily | Max 2–3 times a week |

---

### Meal Ideas

**Breakfast (after workout)**
- 3 boiled / scrambled eggs + 1–2 rotis or multigrain bread
- Poha with peanuts + 2 boiled eggs on the side
- Oats cooked in milk + banana + handful of peanuts
- Chicken sandwich (multigrain bread, leftover chicken, veggies)

**Lunch**
- Dal + sabzi + 1–2 rotis
- Chicken curry / dry + 1 roti or small bowl of rice
- Egg bhurji + 2 rotis
- Fish curry + small portion of rice

**Dinner (lighter than lunch)**
- Dal + 1 roti + sabzi
- Chicken stir-fry with veggies (10 min recipe)
- 2 eggs + sabzi + roti
- Soup on light nights

**Snacks**
- Roasted chana or peanuts (palm-size)
- 1 banana or seasonal fruit
- Chaas (plain or salted)
- 1–2 boiled eggs

---

### Plate Method (no calorie counting needed)

```
[ Half plate: vegetables / dal / sabzi ]
[ Quarter plate: protein (eggs / chicken / fish / paneer) ]
[ Quarter plate: carbs (rice / roti) ]
```

Just reduce the carb quarter by ~25% from what you normally serve yourself.

**Water target:** 2.5–3 litres daily. Keep a 1L bottle on your desk, finish it at least twice during work.

---

### Morning Routine (after shower, 5 min)

1. Wash face gently with face wash
2. Pat dry — don't rub
3. Thin layer of moisturizer
4. Sunscreen — yes, even indoors (windows + screen exposure count)

### Night Routine (before sleep, 3 min)

1. Wash face — removes the day's oil and dirt
2. Thin layer of moisturizer
3. Cold water or cold spoon on under-eye area — 60 seconds (reduces dark circles, completely free)

---

### What Actually Improves Skin & Dark Circles

| Factor | What to do |
|--------|-----------|
| Sleep deprivation | Most important fix — follow the sleep shift plan |
| Dehydration | Hit 2.5L water daily |
| Screen at night | Phone away by 11:30 PM |
| Excess salt | Cut packaged snacks |
| Touching face | Stop — hands carry oil and bacteria |
| No sunscreen | Apply daily, skipping makes pigmentation worse |
